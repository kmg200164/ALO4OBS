## Lucide icons

All UI icons use the official **lucide-static 0.468.0** stock set:
menu, github, bug, heart, settings, book-open, languages, sun, moon, palette,
play, trash-2, maximize, rotate-ccw, and save.

The original SVG glyphs are embedded inline with their 24 × 24 viewBox and
default stroke width 2. Header icons display at 32 × 32; action icons at 24 × 24.
Only display size, CSS classes, and accessibility attributes are adapted.
No icon package is required at runtime.
